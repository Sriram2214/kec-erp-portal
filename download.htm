Kings Engineering College, established in 2001, is a Christian Minority Self-Financing institution approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, and affiliated with Anna University, Chennai.

Vision

The Vision of Kings Engineering College is to provide industry relevant technical education, motivate our technocrats to carry out skillful research and encourage innovation and thereby uplift our society through technology.

Mission

  • Provide high quality technical education in the major engineering disciplines through a creative balance of academic, professional and extracurricular programs.

  • Prioritize quality teaching and adopt a variety of approaches and methods to grow upon current research and multiple theories of teaching and learning.

  • Guide the student to earn secure careers and become a trendsetter.

  • Sponsor and educate less privileged with quality technical education.

Management

The college is managed by the Chartian Educational and Health Trust, a registered public trust for education and charitable purposes, under the aegis of the Rajam Group of Companies, Chennai, which also oversees Queens Land Amusement Park and the star-category Pleasant Days Hotel.

Objectives of the Trust

  • Establish the college as a unique hub of excellence in higher education, dedicated to total quality management across all levels.

  • Elevate the techno-social standing of young individuals through a range of undergraduate engineering degree programs.

  • Commit to uplifting economically disadvantaged community members, improving their financial status through generous assistance.

Dean (Academics)

Elevating Education with an Academic Maestro: Guiding Minds, Shaping Futures, and Architecting Knowledge with Intellect in Action.

Kings Engineering College has successfully completed two decades of dedicated service in educating rural and economically disadvantaged students in the field of Engineering. With the globalization of the Indian economy, there is a growing need for world-class quality in products and services to compete with global competitors in both manufacturing and service sectors. This presents numerous challenges and opportunities for engineers in India and abroad. In this changing landscape, Engineering Institutions must transform from being mere teaching institutions to becoming centers of knowledge creation, innovation, and technology implementation. They must focus on transferring technology to practical applications, particularly for rural development, to address regional imbalances and provide urban amenities in rural areas. In addition to the prescribed syllabus, it is imperative for students to demonstrate a willingness to learn, develop problem-solving skills, and possess effective interpersonal communication and relationship-building abilities, and exhibit motivation for work and cultural sensitivity.
